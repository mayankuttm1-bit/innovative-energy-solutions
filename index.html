Innovative Energy Solutions registers your application directly on the National Portal for Rooftop Solar. Because your system is connected to the grid via net-metering, any extra units produced during sunny summer months offset any shortfall during the rainy season.
A net meter is a bi-directional electricity meter installed by MPPKVVCL (Madhya Pradesh Paschim Kshetra Vidyut Vitaran Company). It records both the electricity you import from the grid and the excess solar electricity you export to the grid. You are only billed for the "net" difference. Innovative Energy Solutions manages 100% of the Discom approvals, meter testing, and installation on your behalf.
